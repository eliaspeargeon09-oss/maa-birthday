# 💌 Website Text & Sentences Central Editor

Welcome! Every single sentence, quote, prayer, letter, message, button label, and title on the entire website is centralized right here in this **`src/content/`** folder.

## 📁 Files in this Folder

### 1. **`siteContent.ts`** (Main Hub)
Edit this file to customize any chapter or scene text:
- **🌐 Global Navigation:** Brand wordmark (`"For Maa"`), progress bar titles, and Next/Back button labels.
- **🎬 Chapter 1 (The Opening Film):** 
  - First sequence lines (3 lines)
  - Second sequence lines (2 lines)
  - Emotional climax two-line title (`"u r always my best"`, `"mom ever and ever"`)
  - Skip button (`"Skip to Message →"`) and continue button.
- **🌿 Chapter 2 (To Our Beloved Maa):** 
  - Badge, title, and subtitle
  - All 6 core attributes surrounding Maa (Your Heart, Your Kindness, Your Strength, Your Smile, Your Presence, Your Faith).
- **💌 Chapter 3 (A Letter for Maa):** 
  - Envelope stamp text, monogram, title, and open button
  - Complete personal letter paragraphs, greeting, closing, and signature.
- **📸 Chapter 4 (Sunlit Memories Keepsake Album):** 
  - Headline, subheading, hover text, and footer prompt.
- **🕊️ Chapter 5 (A Prayer for Maa):** 
  - Prayer introduction and body lines (including your personal edits)
  - Scripture blessing lines and reference.
- **🎂 Chapter 6 (Birthday Countdown & Cake Celebration):** 
  - Reveal message and wish button (`"LET'S MAKE A WISH"`)
  - 5-second countdown subtitle and hint
  - Make a wish prompt (`"CLOSE YOUR EYES AND MAKE A WISH, MAA."`) and ready button
  - Candle blowing out breeze text
  - Cake cutting title (`"NOW, LET'S CUT YOUR BIRTHDAY CAKE!"`)
  - Grand celebration greeting (`"HAPPY BIRTHDAY, MAA!"`), warm blessings, and replay button.
- **👑 Chapter 7 (The Grand Birthday Reveal):** 
  - Birthday greeting, name, portrait caption, tribute paragraphs, and replay/restart buttons.
- **🎁 Special Reasons Cards:** 
  - Card titles, quotes, revealed tags, and progress messages.
